HOME INSURANCE POLICY

SECTION 1 – ELIGIBILITY

1.1 Eligibility
The policy is available to individuals aged 18 or older whose primary residence is located in Luxembourg.

1.2 Covered Property
The policy covers the insured person's primary residence and personal belongings located within the residence.


SECTION 2 – COVERAGE

2.1 Fire Damage
Damage caused directly by fire, smoke, or explosion is covered up to the insured value of the property.

2.2 Water Damage
Accidental water damage caused by leaking pipes, household appliances, or internal plumbing systems is covered.

A deductible of EUR 500 applies to each water damage claim.

For exclusions related to water damage, see Section 4.2.

2.3 Theft
Loss or damage resulting from burglary or attempted burglary is covered when there is visible evidence of forced entry.


SECTION 3 – CLAIMS

3.1 Claim Notification
The policyholder must report a claim within five business days after discovering the damage.

3.2 Required Documents
Claims must include photographs of the damage, proof of ownership where applicable, and any relevant police report.


SECTION 4 – EXCLUSIONS

4.1 General Exclusions
Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder is not covered.

Damage resulting from war, nuclear events, or confiscation by public authorities is excluded.

4.2 Water Damage Exclusions
Water damage caused by gradual deterioration, poor maintenance, or flooding from natural disasters is not covered.

Damage caused by pipes that were known to be defective before the incident is also excluded.


SECTION 5 – CANCELLATION

5.1 Cancellation by the Policyholder
The policyholder may cancel the contract by providing 30 days' written notice.

5.2 Cancellation by the Insurer
The insurer may terminate the contract after a fraudulent claim or non-payment of premiums.
